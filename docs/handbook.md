
# Employee Handbook

## Annual Leave

Employees receive 24 days of annual leave per calendar year.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per calendar year.

## Work From Home

Employees can work remotely 3 days per week.

## Notice Period

The standard notice period for employees is 30 days.

## Internet Reimbursement

Employees receive an internet reimbursement of 1000 rupees per month.
